Computer Science & Algorithms

Sorting, searching, and combinatorial optimization all revolve around factorials and binomial coefficients.

  • Sorting n items explores up to n! permutations — 52! ≈ 8×10⁶⁷ possible orderings of a deck of cards.
  • Brute-force TSP on 20 cities: 20! ≈ 2.4×10¹⁸ routes to check.
  • Feature selection: choosing 5 features from 20 candidates = C(20,5) = 15504 combinations.

AI & Machine Learning

Bayesian inference, model selection, and generative models rely heavily on Gamma and Beta functions.

  • Bayesian A/B testing: after 8 successes and 4 failures, your belief about the conversion rate follows Beta(8,4).
  • Variational inference minimizes KL divergence using Gamma(z) as the normalizing kernel of exponential-family posteriors.
  • Cross-validation: C(10,3) = 120 ways to pick 3 test folds from 10.

Statistics & Data Science

Hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, and Bayesian posteriors are all built on Gamma and Beta.

  • The chi-squared distribution with k degrees of freedom is a scaled Gamma(k/2). For k=10: Gamma(5) = 24.
  • A 95% Bayesian credible interval for a proportion uses Beta(α,β) quantiles.
  • Permutation tests: the number of ways to assign 12 subjects to two groups of 6 = C(12,6) = 924.

Physics & Engineering

Quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, and signal processing use Gamma and double factorials constantly.

  • Wick's theorem in QFT: contractions of n fields involve (n-1)!! pairings. For 7 fields: 7!! = 105.
  • Volume of a 6-dimensional unit sphere: V₆ = π³/Gamma(4) = π³/6 ≈ 5.1677.
  • Quantum harmonic oscillator: energy level n has n!! normalization factors.

Biology & Bioinformatics

Sequence analysis, phylogenetics, and population genetics all use generalized factorials.

  • DNA sequence permutations: the number of distinct orderings of 10 nucleotides = 10! = 3,628,800.
  • K-mer counting: choosing 3 positions from a 20-mer = C(20,3) = 1140 possible 3-mers.
  • Allele frequency models use the Beta distribution as a conjugate prior for binomial likelihoods.

Everyday Life

Lottery odds, seating arrangements, and probability estimates all involve these functions.

  • Lottery odds: picking 6 numbers from 49 gives C(49,6) = 13,983,816 possible tickets.
  • Seating 8 guests around a round table: (8-1)! = 7! = 5040 arrangements.
  • Estimating a 30% success rate with 10 trials: your prior belief follows Beta(3,7).
